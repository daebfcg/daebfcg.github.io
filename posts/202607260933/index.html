Strap Buttons Installed

2026-7-26 14:33

Finally got around to installing strap buttons.

These are the DAddario Eliptical End Pins. They are designed to keep the strap from coming loose, and they look cool. A nice, USA made product.

Installation was a little nerve-wracking getting the position just right and the hole drilled straight and to the right depth.

No problem - once screwed in they are solid and look nice.

It is a little confusing which way they go - pointing sideways or up and down. I think it depends on the type of strap, and how the end-slot that goes over the button gets oriented while standing. You want the strap slot to be at 90 degrees to the length-wise ellipse of the button.

Ultimately, the strap buttons can be rotated as needed.

I can now say that this guitar is done.
